# Grilling 2026-10-26: feature 5, the first visit

What the `/grill-with-docs` session of 2026-10-26 decided about feature 5 (a vet signs in, registers a walk in client and pet, records the weight, writes and completes the visit note, invoices it, and takes a payment), and the edits those decisions imply outside `GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/adr/`. Its inputs were the spike note (`2026-10-26-better-auth-spike.md`), specs 0001 and 0002, `docs/domain.md`, `docs/design.md`, and three fact finding passes of the same day: the code on `main`, Better Auth 1.7.12's sources, and the design system artifact (version 22, `1793023211-0d6a`). Rounds are recorded as they settle. Tick a line when it has been applied.

## Round 1: the boundary (settled)

- **The spike's four answers stand.** One username field, `handle@practice-slug`, with a validator (the screen may still show two boxes); the nine `control` tables written by hand in the control migration set (`uuid`, `timestamptz`), the CLI's output diffed against them on every Better Auth bump; `auth check schema` in CI beside `db:check`, with no database; Drizzle rc.5 on the adapter's non join path, `experimental.joins` off.
- **The medium loop.** `/to-spec` and `/to-tickets` follow in this window once the frontier is empty; one worktree per ticket.
- **Staff logins come from operator commands.** `provision-practice` creates the first manager's login itself (today it takes `--manager-user-id` made elsewhere), and a new `add-staff` command creates any other (the demo vet); both go through one staff service that writes the login, the membership and the `staff_member` mirror together. Feature 15 puts the manager's screen on the same service.
- **TOTP and PIN switching move to feature 15**, designed there beside the staff screens and the manager only actions they guard. Feature 5 has no manager only action, the platform admin works through commands, and no real client data enters before go live (OPEN-13). Feature 5 resolves `ctx.actor` as the session's user in one function, so PIN switching later changes only that function. Amends spec 0001's Auth row and its follow-up line (ADR).
- **Four slice 1 tables move to feature 12**: `patient_problem`, `patient_alert`, `encounter_diagnosis` and `encounter_amendment`; feature 12's done when already names the problem list (CR-6) and amendments (CR-1). Feature 5 builds `client`, `client_contact`, `species` (seeded), `breed`, `patient`, `ownership` (AC-5), `care_relationship` (AC-14), `encounter` (AC-2's freeze), `clinical_observation`, `service`, `invoice` with its numbering, `invoice_line`, `ledger_entry` and `client_balance` (AC-4), `payment`, `payment_allocation`; feature 12 proves AC-2's amendment half. Amends spec 0002's build plan, slice 1 line (same ADR).
- **The money cut.** One invoice per visit; service lines from the price list only (no typed lines, no discounts); draft lines removable; issue takes the gapless number and writes the charge ledger entry; one or more payments against that invoice, cash or card, each up to the unpaid remainder; what stays unpaid is the client's Balance due (BL-5, BL-6). Feature 8 takes overpayment and prepayment, payment by transfer, one payment across several invoices, void and refund, and discounts.
- **The price list** is loaded by an operator command from a file (three names and a price in minor units), safe to rerun; a price change never touches past lines. The manager's price list screen stays in feature 15.
- **A visit starts without an Arrival.** It starts from the patient, the button reads New visit (never Walk-in, which the glossary defines as an Arrival with no appointment), and no Arrival is recorded; feature 11 adds the arrival and links visits through `encounter.arrival_id`, its nullable forward column. The visit's client is the patient's current owner, also recorded as the reporter of the reason and the history (CR-5); feature 5 records owners only, no authorised persons.
- **Vital signs** are recorded inside the visit by whoever is on it: weight (kg, to the gram), temperature (°C), pulse and respiratory rate (per minute), each with value, unit, time and measurer (CR-3), a correction a new row (PT-2). Body condition and other kinds later; weighing at reception before the visit (an observation with no encounter) comes with the queue in feature 11.
- **The invoice lives on the visit page**, as the design system's VisitPage draws it: a Draft card with "Add a service or product" and the invoice table. The draft invoice is created when the first service is added, carrying the visit, patient and client ids as references; billing reads nothing clinical (BL-2), and no event mechanism arrives before slice 2's dispensing. The page's primary action is Complete visit, then Take payment once completed; before completion Take payment is a secondary action, so invoice and note stay independent.
- **Who may do what** (one matrix in `packages/contracts` beside the roles, checked by the API on every route; the database still enforces CR-2):

  | Action | Roles |
  |---|---|
  | Register and edit clients and patients | receptionist, assistant, veterinarian, manager |
  | Record weight | receptionist, assistant, veterinarian |
  | Record the other vital signs, write the draft note | assistant, veterinarian |
  | Start a visit (as its veterinarian), complete it (CR-2), cancel a draft | veterinarian |
  | Invoice and take payment | receptionist, assistant, veterinarian, manager |
  | Read what feature 5 shows | every role but groomer |

- **The note** is six plain text sections (multi line, no formatting) in domain.md §7.1's order, labels from the translation files (OPEN-10 decides labels only); Reason for visit is required to complete it.
- **A note survives a dropped connection.** The browser mirrors the draft to IndexedDB on every change (debounced about a second); the server save runs once the text has been still a few seconds, at most every 30 seconds per visit, and on leaving the page and before Complete visit (reconciling spec 0001's "every few seconds" with spec 0002's "at most every 30 seconds"); a save carries the row's version, and a stale one (412) keeps the local text and says someone else changed the note, in a new message added to the design system's `messages.md` (its `error.conflict` banner tells the person to refresh and that their changes were lost, which is wrong here); on reopening, a local copy newer than the server's is offered back. The mirror is per person and per visit, deleted once the server holds the same text and on sign out (clinical text on a shared computer). The note's status line shows saving, saved, or offline with changes kept on this computer (`VisitNote`'s `saveState`).
- **People land on Visits today** until the schedule exists (feature 11): a list page of today's visits at the location (patient, client, vet, draft or completed, invoice state none, draft, unpaid, part paid, paid), New visit its primary action; a draft visit is reopened from it.
- **A returning client is found by exact phone number** (normalised to E.164, sent in a POST body), the same lookup that warns of a duplicate when registering (warned, never blocked); name search arrives with feature 12.
- **Registration** keeps spec 0002's checks: a person client (organisations later) with first and last name required, patronymic optional, one phone required, preferred language defaulting to the practice's; a patient with name, species and sex required, and breed (added inline by the clinic, named in the current language), birth date or an estimated one, neutered, colour and microchip optional; the owner link from today written with the patient; species seeded as spec 0002 lists them (OPEN-11). The interviews may relax the last name rule.
- **Tests** (high stakes: tests first, reviewed before merging): the database rules against the compose Postgres (AC-2, AC-4, AC-5, AC-14, IN-1 on the new tables); an API walk through the real Hono app against the compose Postgres (sign in, register, visit, vital signs, complete, invoice, issue, part payment, Balance due; the matrix's refusals; a foreign practice id refused), the repo's first API test against a database; the pages in jsdom in three languages with axe. The browser walk end to end is done by hand in each language and recorded on the last ticket; Playwright waits until a browser flow is worth automating.

## Round 2: the screens and their rules (settled)

- **The sign in page** has Clinic code, Username and Password; the app joins the first two into `handle@code`, and the computer remembers the clinic code after a successful sign in. The language choice stays; "Forgot your password?" becomes a line asking the manager to reset it (staff have no email). The artifact's SignInPage example and `error.signin` (which speaks of email, and still never says which field was wrong) change to match (ADR 0005: a page's order of parts is the artifact's). "Keep me signed in" is decided with the session's length.
- **New visit** opens a form page that starts with the phone: a known phone shows its client and their patients to pick from, with New patient; an unknown phone opens the client and patient fields on the same page; the primary action, Start visit, saves what is new and opens the visit page in one request. Roles that cannot start a visit get the same page ending in Save; a vet reaches that patient later by phone through New visit.
- **The visit page** in feature 5: the patient header (name, species, sex, age, owner and phone; no alerts until feature 12), the note with its four vital signs and six sections (the artifact's example shows five; History goes back between Reason for visit and Examination), the last recorded weight and its date beside the weight field, and the invoice card. Not yet: the side column (previous visits and vaccinations, features 12 and 13), Print (feature 14), range flags on vital signs (they need per species ranges, PT-3).
- **Cancel visit** is offered on a draft visit only; it discards the visit's draft invoice with it, and is refused once the invoice is issued (feature 8's void is the way out).
- **Issue and pay.** The invoice card has Issue invoice, for a client who pays nothing today. Take payment opens a dialog over the visit page: it issues a draft invoice and records the payment in one transaction; the amount is prefilled with the unpaid remainder, a smaller one is a part payment; cash or card; manat with up to two decimals, stored in qəpik (IN-4); timed now, no back dating. Take payment stays while a remainder is unpaid. Amount received and change for cash wait for feature 8's till.
- **The location** travels in a header on every location scoped request, checked by the API against the practice's active locations; the web app sends the practice's default location; a location switch comes with a second location (deferred).
- **Better Auth's deployment settings** come from the environment, validated with zod at startup: `baseURL` and `trustedOrigins` from `PUBLIC_ORIGIN`, the secret from `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`, secure cookies whenever the origin is https. They are proven at the hosted deploy (deferred).
- **Words** arrive per ticket: each ticket adds what its screens need to the artifact's `glossary.md` and `messages.md` first (three languages, `voice.md`'s rules), publishes, then adds the `packages/i18n` keys. Owed today: Clinic code, Username, the account entry; New visit, Start visit, Complete visit as a button, Cancel visit, Issue invoice, Take payment, Visits today; the note's saving, saved and offline kept states, restoring a draft, the note conflict that keeps the text; sign in failures without email; payment amount errors (zero, above the remainder); completion blocked by a missing reason; field errors for temperature, pulse and respiratory rate. `VisitNote`'s "Saving…" breaks `voice.md`'s no ellipsis rule.
- **Terms**: Practice code (shown as Clinic code) and Handle (shown as Username) in `GLOSSARY.md`'s holding section; a person at two practices has two logins and is two staff members.

## Round 3: the auth mechanics and the cut (settled)

The facts behind this round were read in Better Auth 1.7.12's sources (`npm pack`) and the uncertain ones run against it on a memory adapter on 2026-10-26: its rate limiter keys by `ip|path` and gives sign in paths 3 requests per 10 seconds, with no lockout for passwords (`@better-auth/core` `utils/ip.mjs`, `better-auth` `api/rate-limiter/index.mjs`); `session.disableSessionRefresh` fixes a session's span; `createOrganization` takes no id, while `organizationHooks.beforeCreateOrganization` may return one; several roles join with commas and grant an action when any one role allows it; `/update-user` lets a user change their own username unless `immutableUsername` is set; `disableSignUp` closes `/sign-up/email` while the admin plugin's `createUser` still works server side with no session.

- **An account locks after five failed sign ins**, for 15 minutes, counted per username in a `control` table by a `hooks.before` and `hooks.after` pair; a success resets it, and the lock never reveals whether the username exists. Better Auth's own limit on the sign in path rises to about 30 a minute per IP, a guard against spraying that a clinic signing in together never meets; the client IP comes from `x-forwarded-for`, trusted from kamal-proxy only. An operator command unlocks a login until feature 15 gives the manager the button. This is spec 0001's per account limit and the design system's `error.signin` promise.
- **A session lasts 12 hours from sign in**, never extended (`disableSessionRefresh`), cookie cache off; sign out ends it at once. A session that ends mid note shows `error.session` and the sign in page, and the note's local copy stays for the same person (only sign out deletes it). "Keep me signed in on this computer" leaves the sign in page until PIN switching (feature 15).
- **Provisioning issues the practice id** (the registry row first, as today), creates the manager's login through `auth.api.createUser` (server side, no session), then the organization through `auth.api.createOrganization` as a system action for that user, with `beforeCreateOrganization` taking the id from the registry by the practice code (no registry row, no organization). Better Auth writes its own rows, the creator's membership with role `manager` among them; every step checks before it acts, so a rerun completes what is missing. This closes the spike's open point.
- **Roles**: `control.member.role` (the roles comma joined, Better Auth's own form) is the truth; the staff service writes it and `staff_member.roles` (the array the triggers read, CR-2) in one transaction, and a test checks they agree for every member. The API reads the roles with the membership on every request and checks the route against the matrix in `packages/contracts`, never Better Auth's access control, which keeps only the five role names so that its own checks refuse anything else.
- **Better Auth's endpoints are an allowlist**: sign in by username, sign out, get session; everything else under `/api/auth` answers 404 from a Hono middleware in front of its handler, so a Better Auth bump that adds a route fails closed. `disableSignUp`, `immutableUsername: true`, `allowUserToCreateOrganization: false`. Commands keep calling `auth.api` server side; memberships, roles, names, passwords and the language preference change only through vetora's services and routes.
- **Passwords**: `provision-practice` and `add-staff` set a new login's password and a `reset-password` command replaces one, each read from a hidden prompt, never an argument; the operator runs them with the person present, who types their own; at least 10 characters. Changing one's own password and a manager's reset come with feature 15.
- **The account entry**: the person's language is stored on their login (`az`, `ru` or `en`, validated, defaulting to the practice's default); the sign in page's choice is the computer's, and once someone signs in their own wins. The rail's footer becomes the account entry: the person's name, a language choice saved to their login through a vetora route, and Sign out.
- **`GET /api/me`** is the app's first call after sign in: the person's id, name and language, their practice (id, name, code) with their roles, and its active locations with the default marked. The app builds `/api/p/:practiceId/…` and the location header from it and never relies on Better Auth's active organization (the spike's finding 12). The request context refuses a practice whose registry status is not active or whose schema is behind, with a problem response; the registry cache lives at most 30 seconds (spec 0001).

### The cut `/to-tickets` publishes: six tracer bullets, tests first

1. **Sign in**: the hand written `control` tables with the lockout table; Better Auth configured as above; the staff service; `provision-practice` creating the manager and the organization; `add-staff`, `reset-password` and the unlock command; the roles and the matrix in `packages/contracts`; `/api/me`; the sign in page; the account entry; an empty Visits today as the landing page; `auth check schema` in CI. 5. **Invoice the visit**: `service` and the price list command; `invoice` with its numbering, `invoice_line`, `ledger_entry`, `client_balance` (AC-4); the invoice card, Issue invoice; `InvoiceTable` in minor units. Demo: issuing leaves Balance due equal to the total.
